What does this do?

Functions are a way to reuse logic, which is generally good practice.
But creating a documented M function can be finicky, and the structure isn't easy to remember.

This tool does the scaffolding for you, allowing you to enter your existing expression and use forms to generate the function declaration and documentation.

Okay, so Power Query is the data-cleaning bit of Excel and Power BI. You write a let … in block that does something useful, and may want to make it reusable.

By inserting your block into the form, you can easily generate a documented function.
